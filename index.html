FinMate

Your money. Your records. Your control.

FinMate is a personal finance companion designed to help users manage financial calculations, maintain Khata records and keep notes in one convenient app.

Features

  • Interest and financial calculations
  • Khata and transaction records
  • Personal notes
  • Optional Google Drive backup and restore

FinMate is a personal record-keeping tool, not a bank or financial institution.
