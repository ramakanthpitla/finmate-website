FinMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Information and storage

FinMate helps users maintain financial calculations, Khata records and personal notes. Information entered into the app may be stored locally on the user's device.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

If a user chooses Google Sign-In and enables cloud backup, FinMate uses Google authentication to identify the account and access the Google Drive app-data area for the app's backup and restore features. Backup data may be stored in that area and associated with the user's Google account.

Google account information and Drive data are handled to provide the requested sign-in, backup and restore features. FinMate does not ask users to provide their Google password directly to the app.

Sharing and security

FinMate does not sell users' personal information. Users should protect access to their devices and Google accounts. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data deletion

Users can remove local app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, subject to their device's behavior. Cloud backups may need to be deleted separately from the app-data area through the app's supported features or by contacting the developer.

Children

FinMate is not specifically directed at children.
